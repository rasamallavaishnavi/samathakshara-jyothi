Our Mission

Samathakshara Jyothi Seva Samithi works to create positive social change through education, unity, compassionate service and community empowerment.

🔭

Our Vision

To contribute to a society where every individual has dignity, opportunity and access to the resources needed to lead a meaningful life.

Our Values

Equality

Respect and opportunity for every person.

Integrity

Responsible, transparent and sincere service.

Compassion

Listening to community needs and responding with care.

Samathakshara Jyothi Seva Samithi

Working towards an equal, compassionate and brighter future through service, education and community empowerment.
